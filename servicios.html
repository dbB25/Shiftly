Tarjetas de Vocabulario

Sustantivo

1. Resilience

Traducción: Resiliencia / Capacidad de adaptación

Definición: The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness.

Ejemplos de uso:

  • "Her resilience helped her overcome every obstacle."
  • "Building resilience is key to personal growth."
Sustantivo

2. Breakthrough

Traducción: Avance / Descubrimiento importante

Definición: A sudden, dramatic, and important discovery or development.

Ejemplos de uso:

  • "Scientists have made a major medical breakthrough."
  • "This idea was the breakthrough we needed."
